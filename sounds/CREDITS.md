# Sound credits

The sampled instruments in this folder come from the **Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont**
by Frank Wen, released by its author under the MIT license, as pre-rendered by
Benjamin Gleitzman's **midi-js-soundfonts** project (https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts),
which distributes them under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license.
Rose Studio uses a subset of notes (every third semitone) and pitches between them.

# Rose loop library — credits

Every loop here is released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain). You can use them in commercial releases with no credit required; credit is listed as thanks.

- **microlag** and **Fugue State Audio** — from the [Stargate DAW sample pack](https://github.com/stargatedaw/stargate-sample-pack) (CC0 1.0).
- **Sonic Pi loops** — from [Sonic Pi](https://github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi/tree/dev/etc/samples), originally from freesound.org users godspine, Garzul, mika55, Snapper4298, nemaavla, lezaarth and hullum (CC0 1.0). The two Amen-break loops are deliberately not included: they're sampled from a commercial 1969 record.

Converted to 160 kbps MP3 for size; BPM and key come from the original file names (Sonic Pi tempos measured from loop length).
